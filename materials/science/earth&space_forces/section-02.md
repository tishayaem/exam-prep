## What we learned

We explored how the **Earth spins on its axis**, causing **day and night**. We also learned how the **Sun appears to move across the sky**, even though it is the Earth that is rotating.

## Topic notes

### Why do shadows change?
As the Sun appears to move:
- Shadows are **long** in the morning
- **Shortest** at midday
- **Long** again in the afternoon

This is because the **angle of sunlight** changes.
