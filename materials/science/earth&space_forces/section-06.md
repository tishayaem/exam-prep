## What we learned

We created a **fruit solar system** to understand the *huge* differences in planet sizes and distances.

## Topic notes

### Fruit model sizes
- **Sun** — (not a fruit, but huge!)
- **Mercury** — peppercorn
- **Venus** — cherry tomato
- **Earth** — cherry tomato
- **Mars** — blueberry
- **Jupiter** — watermelon
- **Saturn** — large grapefruit
- **Uranus** — large apple
- **Neptune** — orange

### Distances to scale
If the **Earth is 1 metre** from the Sun:
- Mercury is closest to the sun
- Venus slightly closer than Earth
- Mars slightly further away than Earth
- Jupiter is *much* further away
- Neptune is **very** far away!

This helped us understand that **space is mostly empty space — and VERY big!**
