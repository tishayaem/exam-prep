## What we learned

We explored how different forces **slow objects down**.

## Topic notes

### Friction
- Happens when **two surfaces rub together**
- Slows things down

### Air resistance
- A force caused by **air pushing against a moving object**
- Slows falling or moving objects

### Water resistance
- A force caused by **water pushing against movement**
- Slows objects moving through water

## Vocabulary

| Word             | Meaning                                |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Friction         | A force between two surfaces           |
| Air resistance   | A force caused by air                  |
| Water resistance | A force caused by water                |

## Original questions (Try It)

1. What is friction?
2. What force slows down falling objects?
3. Why do swimmers wear smooth suits?
4. What force slows boats down?

## Notes / flags for Phase 2 enrichment

- Q3 is a beautiful **applied-reasoning** question — answer: "to reduce water resistance, so they can swim faster." Tag `reasoning: true`.
- Q4 has two acceptable answers: "water resistance" (primary) and arguably "friction" (true for hulls scraping). Be lenient.
- Common trap to use as MCQ distractor: confusing "air resistance" with "gravity" for falling objects (both apply; air resistance *slows*, gravity *accelerates*).
- Strong **everyday-life applied questions** to add as variants: "Why are cars streamlined?", "Why is ice slippery?", "Why does a parachute slow a skydiver?"
- Vocabulary → 3 bidirectional flashcards.
