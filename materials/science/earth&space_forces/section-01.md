## What we learned

We learned that the **Earth, Sun and Moon are all spherical** — not flat. People once believed the Earth was flat, but we now have **strong scientific evidence** that all three bodies are roughly spherical.

## Topic notes

### How do we know they are spherical?
- **Photographs from space** clearly show curved surfaces.
- **Lunar eclipses** show the Earth's round shadow on the Moon.
- **Ships disappear hull-first** over the horizon.
- **Time zones** only make sense on a curved Earth.
