## What we learned

We learned the **order of the planets** and the difference between the **rocky planets** and the **gas giants**.

## Topic notes

### Order of the planets (from the Sun)
1. Mercury
2. Venus
3. Earth
4. Mars
5. Jupiter
6. Saturn
7. Uranus
8. Neptune

### Rocky planets
Mercury · Venus · Earth · Mars

### Gas giants
Jupiter · Saturn · Uranus · Neptune

## Vocabulary

| Word         | Meaning                                       |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Planet       | A large body orbiting a star                  |
| Asteroid     | A rocky object orbiting the Sun               |
| Gas giant    | A large planet made mostly of gas             |
| Rocky planet | A planet with a solid surface                 |
