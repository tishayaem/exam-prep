## What we learned

We explored **gravity**, the force that pulls objects towards each other. **EVERYTHING** has its own gravity — even you! But the Earth is a **LOT** bigger and so its gravity is considerably greater.

## Topic notes

### Key ideas
- Gravity pulls objects towards the **centre of the Earth**.
- Gravity keeps **planets in orbit** around the Sun.
- Gravity keeps the **Moon in orbit** around the Earth.
- **Sir Isaac Newton** helped explain gravity.
- *Mr Hutchings insists that Isaac Newton **INVENTED** gravity…!* *(class in-joke)*

## Vocabulary

| Word    | Meaning                                          |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Gravity | A force that pulls objects together              |
| Mass    | How much matter something has                    |
| Weight  | The force of gravity on an object                |
| Orbit   | A curved path caused by gravity                  |

## Original questions (Try It)

1. What is gravity?
2. Who helped explain gravity?
3. What keeps the Moon in orbit?
4. What is the difference between mass and weight?

## Notes / flags for Phase 2 enrichment

- Q2 has a class-joke trap: the answer is **Isaac Newton** (he *explained* it). The materials joke that he "invented" gravity — flag clearly in the explanation so son doesn't internalise the joke as fact.
- **Q4 is hard for a Y5** ("difference between mass and weight"). Expand explanation: mass is *how much stuff* (always the same), weight is *the pull of gravity on that stuff* (changes on the Moon vs Earth).
- Excellent **misconception MCQ distractor**: "Newton invented gravity" (wrong) vs "Newton explained gravity" (right).
- Vocabulary → 4 bidirectional flashcards.
