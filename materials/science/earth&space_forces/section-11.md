## What we learned

**Mechanisms** help us use **smaller forces** to move **bigger loads**.

## Topic notes

### Levers
- A bar that pivots on a **fulcrum**
- Used in seesaws, scissors, crowbars

### Pulleys
- A wheel with a rope
- Helps lift heavy loads — the more pulleys there are, the easier it is — **However** you then have to pull *much more rope* to lift the object. **The more pulleys = the longer the rope!**

### Gears
- Wheels with teeth
- Change speed, direction or force. Just like the gear on your bike.

## Vocabulary

| Word    | Meaning                                            |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Lever   | A bar that pivots to lift a load                   |
| Pulley  | A wheel and rope system                            |
| Gear    | A toothed wheel that transfers force               |
| Fulcrum | The pivot point of a lever                         |
