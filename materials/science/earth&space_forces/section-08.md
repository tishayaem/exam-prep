## What we learned

- The **Sun and stars** are **light sources**.
- The **Moon and planets** do **NOT** make their own light — we see them because they **reflect sunlight**.

## Topic notes

> *Visual on page:* "Light Sources and Reflected Light" diagram — Light Sources column (Sun, Stars) and Reflected Light column (The Moon reflects the Sun's light, Planets reflect the Sun's light, Sunlight reflects off the Moon to Earth).

## Vocabulary

| Word         | Meaning                                       |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Light source | Something that makes its own light            |
| Reflect      | To bounce light off a surface                 |
| Illuminate   | To light up                                   |
