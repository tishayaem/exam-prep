## What we learned

We discovered that the Moon does **not** change shape — it only **looks** different because we see different amounts of the lit half as it orbits the Earth.

The Moon takes **about 28 days** to orbit the Earth. This creates the **lunar month**.

## Topic notes

### Why does the Moon appear to change shape? Because:
- Half the Moon is always lit by the Sun
- As the Moon orbits Earth, we see different amounts of the lit half

> *Visual on page:* "Moon Phases" diagram showing all eight phases (new moon → first quarter → full moon → last quarter → back to new) around the Earth.
