## What we learned

We learned that forces are measured in **newtons (N)** using a **force meter**.

## Topic notes

### Types of forces
- Push
- Pull
- Gravity
- Friction
- Air resistance
- Water resistance

## Vocabulary

| Word         | Meaning                                     |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Force        | A push or pull                              |
| Newton (N)   | The unit used to measure force              |
| Force meter  | A tool used to measure force                |

## Original questions (Try It)

1. What is the unit of force?
2. What tool measures force?
3. Give an example of a push.
4. Give an example of a pull.

## Notes / flags for Phase 2 enrichment

- Q1 expected answer: "newton" / "newtons" / "N". `acceptable[]` must cover all three (the unit symbol matters).
- Q3 / Q4 are open-ended — many valid examples. Rich `acceptable[]` (pushing a door, pushing a swing, pushing a trolley; pulling a rope, pulling open a drawer, etc.).
- Strong **classify-the-action** variant: given an action, is it a push or a pull? (Includes tricky ones — "opening a door towards you" = pull.)
- Vocabulary → 3 bidirectional flashcards.
