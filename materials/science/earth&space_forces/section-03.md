## What we learned

We discovered that:
- The **Earth orbits the Sun** once every **year**.
- The **Moon orbits the Earth** every **28 days**.
- The **Earth is tilted**, and this tilt causes the **seasons**.

## Topic notes

### Why do we have seasons?
Because the Earth is **tilted at 23.5°**, different parts of the Earth receive more or less sunlight during its orbit.

- When the **Northern Hemisphere is tilted towards the Sun → Summer**
- When it is tilted **away → Winter**
