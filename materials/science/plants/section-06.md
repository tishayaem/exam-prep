| Habitat     | The natural home of a plant or animal         |

## Topic notes

### What is a habitat?
A habitat provides everything an organism needs:
- Food
- Water
- Shelter
- Space
- The right temperature
- The right amount of light

*Examples:* woodland, coast, desert, pond, grassland, urban areas.

### What is an adaptation?
Adaptations can be:

**Physical**
- Thick fur
- Sharp claws
- Long roots
- Waxy leaves

**Behavioural**
- Migration
- Nocturnal hunting
- Turning towards the sun
