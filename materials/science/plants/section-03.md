## What we learned

We carefully took apart real flowers to see the different parts inside. This helped us understand how flowers work. We laid them out and took a picture of our work!

## Vocabulary

| Word   | Meaning                                       |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Petal  | Attracts insects                              |
| Sepal  | Protects the bud                              |
| Stamen | Male part of the flower                       |
| Anther | Makes pollen                                  |
| Carpel | Female part of the flower                     |
| Stigma | Sticky surface that catches pollen            |
| Style  | Tube leading to the ovary                     |
| Ovary  | Contains ovules                               |
| Ovule  | Becomes a seed after fertilisation            |

## Topic notes

### What we did
- Used a **scalpel** and **tweezers** to take apart a flower (a lily? Fun — and a little bit dangerous!)
- Found and labelled the parts: **petals, sepals, stamen, carpel**
- Matched each part to its job

> *Visual on page:* fully labelled flower dissection diagram showing petal, sepal, stamen, anther, filament, carpel, stigma, style, ovary, ovule.

## Original questions (Try It)

1. Which part of the flower makes pollen?
2. What is the job of the stigma?
3. Where are the ovules found?
4. Why did we remove the petals first?
