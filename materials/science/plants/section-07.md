## What we learned

We compared two very different habitats: **woodland** and **coastal**. We looked at the conditions in each habitat and how plants and animals are adapted to survive there.

## Vocabulary

| Word        | Meaning                                          |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Woodland    | A habitat with many trees, shade and leaf litter |
| Coastal     | A habitat near the sea, with wind, salt and waves |
| Canopy      | The upper layer of trees                         |
| Understorey | Plants growing beneath the trees                 |
| Shelter     | Protection from weather or predators             |

## Topic notes

### ⭐ Woodland Habitat
**Conditions:** Shady · Damp soil · Lots of leaf litter · Sheltered from wind · Many layers

**Plants:** Bluebells · Ferns · Mosses · Oak, beech, birch

**Animals:** Deer · Foxes · Woodpeckers · Beetles · Squirrels and The Gruffalo *(class joke)*

### ⭐ Coastal Habitat
**Conditions:** Strong winds · Salt spray · Sandy or rocky ground · Changing tides · Little shelter

**Plants:** Sea thrift · Marram grass · Sea kale

**Animals:** Crabs · Gulls · Limpets · Sand hoppers

### ⭐ Comparison Table

| Feature  | Woodland               | Coastal                 |
| -------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Light    | Shady                  | Very bright             |
| Wind     | Sheltered              | Very windy              |
| Soil     | Rich, damp             | Sandy / rocky           |
| Plants   | Shade-tolerant         | Salt-tolerant           |
| Animals  | Camouflaged, climbing  | Hard-shelled, gripping  |

## Original questions (Try It)

1. Why do woodland plants often have large leaves?
2. What might sea water need special adaptations for?
3. Name one woodland animal and one coastal animal.
4. Which habitat has stronger winds, and how does this affect the plants?
