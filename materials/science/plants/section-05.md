## What we learned

We explored how different animals grow and change during their lives. Each species has its own life cycle, but all life cycles show how an organism:

- begins life
- grows
- changes
- reproduces
- and eventually dies

## Vocabulary

| Word          | Meaning                                              |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Life cycle    | The stages an organism goes through from birth to adulthood |
| Metamorphosis | A big change in body form during growth              |
| Larva         | A young form that looks different from the adult     |
| Pupa          | A resting stage where transformation happens         |
| Juvenile      | A young animal that looks like a smaller adult       |

## Topic notes

### ⭐ 1. Frog Life Cycle (Amphibian)
**Stages:**
1. Egg
2. Tadpole
3. Tadpole with legs
4. Froglet
5. Adult frog

### ⭐ 2. Butterfly Life Cycle (Insect)
**Stages:**
1. Egg
2. Caterpillar (larva)
3. Chrysalis (pupa)
4. Adult butterfly

### ⭐ 3. Robin Life Cycle (Bird)
**Stages:**
1. Egg
2. Hatchling
3. Nestling
4. Fledgling
5. Adult robin

### ⭐ 4. Woodlouse Life Cycle (Crustacean)
**Stages:**
1. Eggs
2. Manca (juvenile)
3. Adult woodlouse
