## What we learned

We learned how flowering plants reproduce. This includes pollination, fertilisation, and seed formation. We imagined the journey of a little bit of pollen carried by a bumble bee from one flower to another.

## Vocabulary

| Word          | Meaning                                              |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Pollination   | Pollen moving from one flower to another             |
| Pollen        | Powder made by the anther                            |
| Nectar        | Sweet liquid that attracts insects                   |
| Fertilisation | When pollen joins with an ovule to make a seed       |

## Topic notes

### What is pollination?
Pollination is when pollen moves from one flower to another. This helps plants make seeds.

### How does pollination happen?
- Insects (like the busy bumble bee) visit flowers to collect nectar
- Pollen sticks to their bodies
- When they visit another flower, the pollen rubs off
- This helps the plant make seeds

### Other ways pollination can happen
- Wind (e.g. grass, trees)
- Water (rare)
- Animals (like birds or bats)

### What happens after pollination?
- **Fertilisation** happens inside the flower
- A seed starts to grow
- The flower turns into a **fruit** with seeds inside
