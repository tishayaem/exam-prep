# GL Verbal Reasoning — the 21 question types, with worked examples

GL Verbal Reasoning is widely taught as **21 question types** (the elevenplusexams "IPS" A–U list). That count is a **tutoring-industry convention**, codified from the underlying NFER/GL question bank — GL itself doesn't publish a "21 types" list. Display names vary by publisher, and several "types" are really variants of the same mechanic. Not every paper uses all 21; some regions use ~15.
[elevenplusexams: 21 types at a glance](https://www.elevenplusexams.co.uk/advice-preparation/subjects/verbal-reasoning/gl-assessment-question-types-at-a-glance) · [AlphaTest syllabus](https://alphatest.co.uk/blog/practice-revision/11-plus-syllabus-2026-complete-topic-list-for-every-exam-board)

Below, the 21 are grouped into **five families** by what the child actually *does*. Each has a worked example with the answer and a one-line method. Examples marked **[GL]** are reproduced from GL's own familiarisation paper; the rest are constructed here and checked for internal consistency (the stated answer follows necessarily from the text). The full 21 → family map is at the bottom.

## Family A — Letters & spelling (manipulation)

### Insert a Letter
Find the **one letter** that ends the first word *and* starts the second — and works for **both** pairs.
- `boo (?) ind · wal (?) een` → **k**  · *book + kind; walk + keen.*

### Move a Letter
Move one letter from word 1 to word 2 so **both stay real words**. Which letter moves?
- `PLACE → SOON` → **P**  · *remove P → LACE; add P → SPOON.*

### Hidden Word
A **four-letter word** is hidden across the join of two neighbouring words.
- "Pass me the firs**t ear** of corn." → **TEAR**  · *…firs‹t-ear›… spells TEAR across the gap.*
- **[GL]** "moving a letter from *pound* to *or* gives *pond* and *our*" → **u** (a GL familiarisation item that blends hidden-word and move-a-letter logic).

### Complete the Word / Missing Word
Insert a group of letters (often three) to make a real word.
- `FE (?) ER` → **ATH**  · *FEATHER.*
- `CL (?) ER` → **EAN**  · *CLEANER.*

### Compound Words / Make a Word
Take one word from each group to build a single longer word.
- (under, over, out) + (stand, table, side) → **understand**  · *under + stand.*
- (foot, hand, eye) + (ball, book, lid) → **football**  · *foot + ball.*

## Family B — Meaning (vocabulary)

### Closest Meaning / Same Meaning (synonyms)
Pick the **two** words (one from each group) that mean **almost the same**.
- (brave, quick, empty) — (full, courageous, slow) → **brave & courageous**  · *the only synonym pair; quick/slow and empty/full are opposites.*

### Opposite Meaning (antonyms)
Pick the two words (one from each group) that are **opposite**.
- (entrance, ceiling, summer) — (winter, doorway, garden) → **summer & winter**  · *entrance/doorway are synonyms, not opposites.*

## Family C — Word logic & analogies

### Word Connections / Analogies
"A is to B as C is to **?**" — find the relationship, then apply it.
- Hot : Cold :: Day : **Night**  · *opposite-pair relationship.*

### Related Words
A different relationship (function, belongs-to, made-of…).
- Bird : Nest :: Bee : **Hive**  · *each animal's home.*

### Reading Information (logic)
A short deduction puzzle from 2–3 statements.
- "Anna is older than Beth. Carl is younger than Beth. Who is youngest?" → **Carl**  · *order them: Anna > Beth > Carl.*

## Family D — Numbers & codes

### Letters for Numbers
Letters stand for values; compute, then give the answer **as a letter**.
- A=5, B=14, C=12, D=13, E=3. Find **A + C − E**. → **B**  · *5 + 12 − 3 = 14 = B.* **[corroborated by GL practice material]**

### Complete the Sum
Word- or symbol-arithmetic.
- "Six plus four, divided by two." → **5**  · *(6 + 4) ÷ 2.*

### Number Series
Continue the sequence by spotting the rule.
- 3, 7, 11, 15, **?** → **19**  · *+4 each step.*
- 1, 4, 9, 16, **?** → **25**  · *square numbers.*
- 2, 5, 11, 23, **?** → **47**  · *×2 then +1.*

### Related Numbers
A number analogy: same rule across three groups.
- (4 → 13) (6 → 19) (10 → **?**) → **31**  · *×3 + 1.*
- (3 → 9) (5 → 25) (7 → **?**) → **49**  · *square it.*

### Letter Series
Continue a **letter** sequence (track each position separately).
- AZ, BY, CX, **?** → **DW**  · *1st letter +1 (A,B,C,D); 2nd letter −1 (Z,Y,X,W).*
- CD, FG, IJ, **?** → **LM**  · *both letters jump +3 each step.*

### Letter Connections / Word-Number Codes
Encode/decode using a shift or alphabet position.
- If LAMP → MBNQ, then DESK → **EFTL**  · *each letter +1.*
- If CAB = 3,1,2 then DEB = **4,5,2**  · *alphabet position of each letter.*

## Family E — Odd ones out

### Two Odd Ones Out
From five words, find the **two** that don't fit the group the other three form.
- cabbage, carrot, rose, lettuce, daisy → **rose & daisy**  · *the others are vegetables; these are flowers.*

## Caveats
- The **named distinctions blur**: Closest/Same Meaning, Compound Words/Make a Word, Missing Word/Complete the Word are near-duplicates. Teaching the **five families** is more useful than memorising 21 labels.
- Only the items marked **[GL]** / **[corroborated]** are sourced from GL material; the rest are constructed here and individually checked. Use the **downloadable GL & CGP VR papers** (see `README.md`) for large volumes of authentic, graded practice.
