# GL 11+ Maths — topic-by-topic with verified worked examples

GL Maths is built on the **KS2 National Curriculum** but stretched (more multi-step problems, "greater depth", some early-Year-6 content a Year-5 child may not have met). It's almost entirely **multiple choice** with a separate answer sheet, **no calculator**, typically **~50 questions in ~50 minutes** (≈1 min/question) — so the stretch is in multi-step reasoning, answer-option traps, and speed, not in writing-out. **Every answer below has been arithmetically checked.** "Beyond standard KS2" items are flagged.
[GL free materials](https://11plus.gl-assessment.co.uk/pages/free-materials) · [Atom 11+ maths](https://www.atomlearning.com/blog/11-plus-maths) · [examhappy maths topics](https://examhappy.co.uk/11-plus-maths-topics/) · [GL calculator policy](https://support.gl-education.com/knowledge-base/assessments/ptm-support/on-the-day-of-the-test/what-happens-on-the-day)

## 1. Number & place value (negatives, Roman numerals, rounding)
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| Value of digit 4 in 3,425,718 | **400,000** | hundred-thousands column |
| Round 2,748,361 to nearest 10,000 | **2,750,000** | thousands digit is 6 → round up |
| −6 + 9 − 4 | **−1** | −6+9=3; 3−4 |
| Temp rises −4 °C → 17 °C, by how much? | **21 °C** | 17 − (−4) = 21 |
| MCMXLIV as a number | **1944** | 1000+900+40+4 |
| Whole numbers strictly between −3 and 4 | **6** | −2,−1,0,1,2,3 |

**Traps:** rounding off the wrong column; subtracting across zero (17−(−4)≠13); Roman subtractive pairs (CM=900); internal zeros in place value.

## 2. Four operations & mental arithmetic
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| 345 × 28 | **9,660** | 345×30 − 345×2 |
| 8,526 ÷ 6 | **1,421** | short division |
| 5 + 3 × (8 − 2)² ÷ 4 | **32** | 8−2=6; 6²=36; ×3=108; ÷4=27; +5 |
| 1000 − (248 + 367) | **385** | 248+367=615 |
| 47 × 99 | **4,653** | 47×100 − 47 |

**Traps:** BODMAS order (brackets/indices before ×÷); powers before multiply; decimal alignment.

## 3. Factors, multiples, primes, squares & cubes
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| How many factors has 36? | **9** | 1,2,3,4,6,9,12,18,36 |
| HCF of 24 and 36 | **12** | largest common divisor |
| Primes between 30 and 40 | **31, 37** | others composite |
| √196 + 4³ | **78** | 14 + 64 |
| Bells every 6, 8, 10 s — next together? | **120 s** | LCM |

**Traps:** HCF vs LCM ("ring together" = LCM); 1 isn't prime, 2 is the only even prime; squares vs doubles, cubes vs triples.

## 4. Fractions
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| 2⁄3 + 3⁄4 | **1 5⁄12** | common denom 12: 8⁄12+9⁄12 |
| 3⁄4 of 60 | **45** | 60÷4×3 |
| 5⁄8 − 1⁄3 | **7⁄24** | 15⁄24 − 8⁄24 |
| 2½ × 1⅓ | **3⅓** | 5⁄2 × 4⁄3 = 20⁄6 |
| Cake ¾ full, Tom eats ⅓ of it — fraction of **whole** left? | **½** | eaten ⅓×¾=¼; ¾−¼ |

**Traps:** adding denominators; "fraction of a fraction" = multiply; answer is *of the whole*, not the remainder; simplify the final form.

## 5. Decimals
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| 3.4 + 12.75 + 0.6 | **16.75** | line up points |
| 0.6 × 0.4 | **0.24** | 6×4=24, 2 dp |
| 4.8 ÷ 0.6 | **8** | ×10 both → 48÷6 |
| 0.125 as a fraction | **1⁄8** | 125⁄1000 |
| 2.5 m ribbon into 0.4 m pieces — whole pieces + leftover? | **6 pieces, 0.1 m** | 6×0.4=2.4 |

**Traps:** decimal-place count in ×; dividing by a decimal without scaling; whole-pieces vs raw quotient (6.25).

## 6. Percentages
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| 15% of 240 | **36** | 10%=24, 5%=12 |
| £80 up 25% | **£100** | ×1.25 |
| £60 reduced 30% | **£42** | ×0.70 |
| After 20% off a jacket is £48 — original? *(reverse %, beyond core KS2)* | **£60** | £48 = 80% → ÷0.8 |
| 840 students, 45% boys — girls? | **462** | 55% → ×0.55 |

**Traps:** reverse percentages (treat £48 as 80%, don't take 20% of it); take the complement (girls, not boys); "increase by 25%" (×1.25) vs "is 25% of".

## 7. Ratio & proportion
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| Share £60 in 2:3 | **£24 & £36** | 5 parts → £12 each |
| Recipe 8 people = 200 g flour; for 20? | **500 g** | ×2.5 |
| Blue:red = 2:3, 35 total — red? | **21** | 5 parts → 7 each; 3×7 |
| Map 1:50,000, road 4 cm — real km? | **2 km** | 4×50,000 cm = 2 km |
| 5 workers build in 6 days; 3 workers? *(inverse)* | **10 days** | 30 worker-days ÷ 3 |

**Traps:** mapping a part to the total (÷5 parts, not ÷3); inverse vs direct (more workers = fewer days); cm→km unit slips.

## 8. Algebra (expressions, sequences, function machines)
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| Solve 3x + 7 = 22 | **x = 5** | (22−7)÷3 |
| 4a − 3 when a = 6 | **21** | 24−3 |
| 4, 7, 10, 13… 10th term? *(nth-term, beyond core KS2)* | **31** | 3n+1 |
| Machine "×2 then +5" outputs 23 — input? | **9** | (23−5)÷2 |
| Solve 5y − 4 = 3y + 10 | **y = 7** | 2y = 14 |

**Traps:** reverse the function machine in reverse order; term-to-term (+3) vs position rule (3n+1); sign errors with unknowns on both sides.

## 9. Measurement (length/mass/capacity, money, time)
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| 3,450 g → kg | **3.45 kg** | ÷1000 |
| Film 14:35 + 2 h 20 min ends? | **16:55** | +2h then +20 min |
| 3 books at £4.99, pay £20 — change? | **£5.03** | 14.97; 20−14.97 |
| 250 ml glasses from 2 L? | **8** | 2000÷250 |
| Minutes in 2 h 15 min | **135** | 120+15 |

**Traps:** base-60 time arithmetic; conversion direction (g→kg is ÷1000); 24-hr vs am/pm.

## 10. Geometry — shapes & angles
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| Triangle angles 65°, 72° — third? | **43°** | 180−65−72 |
| Interior angle of regular hexagon *(beyond core KS2)* | **120°** | 180×(6−2)÷6 |
| Angles at a point 90°,130°,75°,x | **65°** | 360−sum |
| Isosceles apex 40° — base angles? | **70°** | (180−40)÷2 |
| Edges of a triangular prism | **9** | 3+3+3 |

**Traps:** 180° (line) vs 360° (point); interior vs exterior angle; assuming isosceles/equilateral when not stated.

## 11. Geometry — perimeter, area, volume
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| Rectangle 12×7 — area & perimeter | **84 cm², 38 cm** | 12×7; 2×(12+7) |
| Triangle base 10, height 6 — area | **30 cm²** | ½×10×6 |
| Cuboid 5×4×3 — volume | **60 cm³** | 5×4×3 |
| L-shape: 10×8 with 4×3 removed — area | **68 cm²** | 80−12 |
| Rectangle area 48, width 6 — perimeter | **28 cm** | length 8; 2×(8+6) |

**Traps:** area vs perimeter (units give it away); triangle area needs ½; compound shapes — subtract the missing piece; do the *final* step asked (perimeter, not the side).

## 12. Coordinates & translation/reflection
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| Midpoint of (2,3) & (8,9) | **(5, 6)** | average each |
| Translate (4,2) by 3 right, 5 down | **(7, −3)** | x+3, y−5 |
| Reflect (5,3) in the y-axis | **(−5, 3)** | x changes sign |
| Reflect (5,3) in the x-axis | **(5, −3)** | y changes sign |
| Square corners (1,1)(1,4)(4,4) — 4th? | **(4, 1)** | complete the square |

**Traps:** (x,y) order; reflecting in the **y**-axis changes the **x**-coordinate (the #1 error here); sign errors in four-quadrant work.

## 13. Statistics (charts, mean/median/mode/range)
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| Mean of 4,7,9,7,3 | **6** | 30÷5 |
| Median of 8,3,5,10 | **6.5** | order → (5+8)÷2 |
| Mean of 5 is 12; add 18 — new mean? | **13** | (60+18)÷6 |
| 6,9,x,5 have mean 7 — find x | **8** | total 28 − 20 |
| Pie chart of 360 students, 90 like football — slice angle? | **90°** | 90⁄360 ×360° |

**Traps:** even-count median = average the two middle values (and **re-order first**); mode/median/mean mix-ups; pie-chart proportion vs angle (whole = 360°); pictogram symbol = >1 item.

## 14. Multi-step word problems & logic
| Q | Answer | Method |
|---|--------|--------|
| 127 children, buses hold 30 — buses needed? | **5** | 127÷30 = 4 r 7 → round **up** |
| £15: spends ⅓ on a book + £2.50 sweets — left? | **£7.50** | 15−5−2.50 |
| Sam is 3× Tom's age, together 32 — Sam? | **24** | 4 parts = 32 → 8 & 24 |
| 3 consecutive numbers sum 72 — largest? | **25** | middle 24 → 23,24,25 |
| Tap fills 2 L per 5 min — fill 30 L? | **75 min** | 15 lots × 5 |

**Traps:** remainder direction ("needed" rounds up; GL offers both 4 and 5 as options); stopping at the intermediate step (answering Tom not Sam); "÷3 gives the middle, not the largest".

## ISEB Common Entrance at 11+ (this child's exam — written format)

The school sets the **written** ISEB format, which is materially different from the GL multiple-choice paper above. The **topic tables in this file still apply** — the maths content is the same KS2-plus syllabus — but the *exam style* differs, and the app's questions should reflect it.

**Format (per ISEB):**
- **One paper, 60 minutes, non-calculator.**
- It opens with **~8 straightforward 1-mark numeracy questions** where the answer alone scores — no working needed.
- Then come **worded questions that require working to be shown**. *If the working isn't shown, marks can be lost*, even when the final answer is right. Neat, clear working and good mental arithmetic are explicitly expected.
- Syllabus spans **number, algebra, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratio, shape & space, measures, data handling, and problem-solving**.
- Stated aims: **fluency, reasoning, investigative thinking, and applying knowledge to unfamiliar problems** — i.e. multi-step word problems, not single-step recall.

**Not to be confused with the ISEB Pre-Test:** the *Pre-Test* is a separate online, adaptive, multiple-choice screen (English, Maths, VR, NVR) used by many senior schools earlier in the process. This child sits the **written** paper, so author **show-your-working, final-answer** items, not pure MCQ.

**Implications for our content (how the maths sections are authored):**
- Favour **multi-step worded `numeric` questions** with a single final answer; the `explanation` should *model the working line by line* (common denominator → multiply → simplify; "parts = …" → one part → answer), because in the real paper that working is where the marks live.
- Keep a spread of **difficulty 1 entry items** (the "first 8" quick numeracy) through **difficulty 3 multi-step** problems.
- Tag provenance: new ISEB-oriented items use `source: 'ISEB CE · …'`; quick numeracy variants use `'11+ Maths · …'`; the original GL-style items keep `'GL Maths · …'`.

**ISEB practice sources (the school's actual papers aren't released):**
- **Galore Park** — the publisher closest to ISEB; *So You Really Want to Learn Maths* + practice papers are the de-facto practice set.
- **ISEB 2016 specimen 11+ maths paper** (free PDF, mirrors the format): <https://www.11plusguide.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/11-mathematics-iseb-2016.pdf>
- **ISEB CE maths overview** (format, aims): <https://www.iseb.co.uk/assessments/common-entrance/mathematics/>
- **Owl Tutors CE maths syllabus** (topic breakdown): <https://owltutors.co.uk/advice/13-plus/maths/syllabus/>
- **Keystone 11+ maths guide**: <https://www.keystonetutors.com/news/11-plus-maths-guide>

## Format & timing (confirm locally)
- **Multiple choice**, separate answer sheet; a minority of schools use a write-in "standard" format.
- **No calculators.**
- Baseline **~50 Qs / 50 min**, increasing difficulty — but **counts/timings vary by region and school**, and an independent school may set its own paper. Confirm with admissions.
